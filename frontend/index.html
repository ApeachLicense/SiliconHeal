6.4.2 N-Bank Crossbar Routing & Coprime Row Stride

On-chip scratchpad has 8 parallel 32-bit banks (Bank = word_idx mod 8) monitored by the instrumented SramTile<float, R, C> proxy. Unpadded SramTile<float, 8, 8> causes all column-transpose lanes to collide on Bank 0.

Coprime Rule: Choose column dimension COLS > TILE_DIM such that gcd(9, 8) = 1 (SramTile<float, 8, 9>) → 0.00% conflict.
