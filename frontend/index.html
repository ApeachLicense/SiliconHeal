4.2.1 64-Byte DMA Alignment & 4.2.2 DMA_CR Reserved Bit

Both DMA_SRC_ADDR and DMA_DST_ADDR MUST be aligned to a 64-byte boundary (alignas(64) at offset [0]). Unaligned transfers trigger ERR_DMA_UNALIGNED_64B.

Section 5.1 · Page 27 CACHE COHERENCY & MMIO

5.1.1 Mandatory hw_dmb() & 5.1.2 Volatile Polling

The HX-9000 DMA engine is non-coherent with CPU L1 D-Cache. Software MUST execute hw_dmb() immediately prior to hw_trigger_dma(). Omitting hw_dmb() leaves 56 dirty L1 cache lines unflushed (Oscilloscope CSV t=418.00us), causing silent tensor CRC32 corruption.

• 5.1.2 Volatile Status Poll: Poll hw_poll_status_volatile() (volatile uint32_t*) to prevent -O2 register-hoisting race conditions.
Section 6.4 · Page 41 (Mutable Bank Spec) 8-BANK INTERLEAVED SRAM

6.4.2 N-Bank Stride Trap & 6.4.3 +1 Element Row Padding

On-chip scratchpad consists of 8 parallel 32-bit banks (Bank = word_idx mod 8). An unpadded tile local_tile[8][8] causes column gathers to hit Bank 0 simultaneously (94.12% conflict), driving Bank 0 sense-amp temperature to 98.4°C (CSV t=1120.50us).

Mandatory Fix (+1 Padding): Declare PADDED_STRIDE = TILE_DIM + 1 (9 floats). Since gcd(9, 8) = 1, column accesses distribute across all banks with 0.00% conflict and cool the die to 62.2°C!
